Design principles

1 · One job per screen, one primary per surface.
At most one filled button in a row, card or panel.
2 · Click opens in place.
An order or person opens the universal side panel. Leaving the page only via "Open full order".
3 · Words first, colour second.
Every status has a label; colour only reinforces it. Terracotta is the brand, never a status.
4 · Show the number that decides.
Who · what · how much · how long · next action + why.
5 · Honest data.
Unknown is "£?" or "not recorded", never £0 or a dash pretending to be a value. Test deals never shown.
6 · Same thing, same name, same place.
One term per concept, one ref format (ORD-245), one ranking shared by Hub, Pipeline and Inbox.
7 · Calm density.
Strict spacing and type scales; align to the grid; nothing floats over content.

Do / Don't

Do
  • Open orders in the side panel.
  • Use one status colour per meaning, with a word.
  • Show "Deposit in · £?" when the amount is unknown.
  • Merge repeated rows: "13 × Waiting for our first reply".
  • Use 4/8/12/16/24/32 spacing only.
Don't
  • Navigate away when a row is clicked.
  • Use terracotta for a warning, or navy for "green".
  • Write REPLY, SOFT FUP, Nothing due.
  • Show £0 or "—" for data we simply don't have.
  • Use raw zinc/amber Tailwind colours or hex in components.

Open decisions for Arin

  1. Status colours — A) one 5-colour status palette separate from the brand accent (recommended) · B) keep navy+terracotta only · C) A, with money in its own amber.
  2. Money vs permit — A) money amber→green; permit blue while with others, amber when our move, green approved (recommended) · B) money its own colour always.
  3. Deposit wording — A) Create invoice → Chase payment → Record payment (recommended) · B) keep Request deposit / Request balance.
  4. "Nothing due" — A) "On track" (recommended) · B) blank · C) "Waiting on …".
  5. Finance cards — A) the 5 above incl. Projected revenue (recommended) · B) current minus Deposits to request · C) 3 cards + lead flow.
  6. Order ref — A) ORD-245 (recommended) · B) ORD-000245 · C) #245.
  7. Hub clean-up lists — A) one collapsed card, full lists in Pipeline (recommended) · B) remove from Hub.
  8. Orphan pages (/priority, /jobs, /payments, /reporting, /workers, /activity, old /pipeline, old /map) — A) delete (recommended) · B) admin-only.
  9. Proof statuses — A) Not received → Received → Sent → Revision → Approved → Lettered, migrate In progress (recommended) · B) keep legacy.
